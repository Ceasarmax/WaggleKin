# WaggleKin: What's Trending in US Pet Products (12 Nov 2026)

These are research findings. "Sales" figures are third-party estimates of Amazon US monthly unit sales (ASINSIGHT, mostly Jun–Aug 2026). The other signals:
- TikTok Shop US data from FindNiche (1 Nov 2026)
- NIQ pet retail data covering the 52 weeks to June 2026
- Petco, PetSmart and Target 2026 holiday lines

Brand names show demand for a *type* of product; we sell unbranded equivalents only.

## Market signals

- **Online pet supplies are growing; stores are shrinking.** Online +17.4%, in-store −2.5% (NIQ).
- **Fastest-growing categories (NIQ):**
  - treat-tossing cameras +32%
  - electronic toys +27%
  - food dispensers +20%
  - dental +17%
  - treat-dispensing toys +15%
  - lick mats +9%
- **Cats are the fastest-growing species**, and cat accessories are accelerating (NIQ).
- **Black Friday is 27 Nov 2026.** Holiday gifts should ship from the **US warehouse**. Keep China "Value Picks" for all-year items.

## Pet food verdict

**No dry or wet food at launch. Never source edible products from China.**
- **Margins:** shipping a 20 lb bag costs $19–43, which wipes out the margin.
- **Competition:** Chewy Autoship makes up 85% of Chewy's sales. Brands also restrict who can sell them (authorised-seller and minimum advertised price policies).
- **Regulation:** you need FDA facility registration if you hold stock, plus state feed licensing.
- **Treats later:** consider 10–20 US-made treats or treat advent calendars through a US distributor, sold as add-ons.
