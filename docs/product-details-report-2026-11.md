# WaggleKin: Product Details, Photos & Accuracy Pass

**Date:** 13 November 2026

**Scope:** every live product on wagglekin.com: 152 CJ Dropshipping products and 43 TopDawg products.

## 1. What was done

| Area | Result |
|---|---|
| **Full supplier details (CJ)** | All 152 live CJ products rewritten from the full CJ listing, each in the same layout: intro, Features, Specifications table, Size guide, How to use, Care, What's in the box, Good to know. Only the sections with source data are shown. |
| **Independent check** | Each of the 152 products was then re-checked by a second agent against CJ's data. 70 were already right; 78 were corrected (unit conversions, invented wording, shipping weights shown as item weights, missing features). The other 3 can't be fixed by us: the fountain has no power details from CJ, and the two photo products have no photo collection (section 2). |
| **Units for US shoppers** | Every measurement is given in both metric and US units, computed rather than estimated: cm/in, kg/lb, g/oz, ml/fl oz. Chinese "jin" sizing is converted too. |
| **Photos added** | 202 useful supplier photos added: size charts, dimension diagrams, how-to graphics and clean product shots. Each was looked at first. Rejected: other sellers' logos, "fast delivery" banners, Chinese text, wrong colours and claim badges. |
| **Misleading photos removed** | 381 photos taken off product galleries. They showed colours, styles, sizes or bundles you don't sell, other brands' logos (VEVOR, TAILUP, petgravity, "The Perfect Part"), "US Seller" banners, health or safety claims, crude text, an unrelated camera tripod, or duplicates. **Nothing was deleted:** every file is still in Shopify → Content → Files and can be re-attached. An undo list is kept. |
| **Main photos fixed** | 46 products had a main (first) image showing something you don't sell. Each now leads with the version you do sell. |
| **Variant photos fixed** | The backpack (TAILUP logo), tactical collar ("US Seller" banner) and cat collar (red box drawn on the photo) now use clean photos for their colour options. |
| **Removed from text** | Supplier names, "eBay", shipping and warehouse promises, Chinese text, "100% brand new" filler, keyword stuffing. Medical claims (anxiety, arthritis, wounds, "vet recommended"). Absolute safety claims ("escape-proof" became "escape-resistant"). "Biodegradable" claims, which carry FTC Green Guides and California risk. Unconfirmed "wool" fibre claims (US wool-labelling law). |
| **TopDawg (45 products)** | Descriptions cleaned and laid out the same way. Removed: broken characters (`_x005F_x000D_`, `??`), disease claims (diabetes, arthritis, heart/kidney, epilepsy), casino spam, unverifiable awards. **No facts were added.** A second check confirmed nothing was invented or lost. |
| **Description styling** | Tables, headings and lists now look clean on phones and desktop. *(This is a theme change, waiting for your OK to go live; see §3.)* |
