# WaggleKin: Local Dropshippers by Country

**Prepared:** 11 November 2026 · **Markets:** US, Canada, UK, Australia, EU/Malta
**Companion to:** [`suppliers.md`](suppliers.md) (global platforms, product costs, compliance and tax)
**Scope:** Research only. Nothing was signed up for, contacted or bought.

> **How this list was built.** One researcher per market searched directories, Shopify App Store listings, local-language queries and supplier websites. A separate fact-checker then tried to *disprove* every candidate: wholesale-only, no stock in the country, no real pet range, dead site, or wrong facts. Only the suppliers that held up are listed below. The ones dropped, and why, are at the end of each section so you don't waste time on them. Most terms come from the suppliers' own pages. Where a fact comes only from a third-party roundup it is marked *(3rd-party)*. **Confirm fees, stock and eligibility for a Malta company with each supplier before you list products.**
>
> The global platforms in `suppliers.md` (CJdropshipping, Syncee, Spocket, Zendrop, DSers, BigBuy, AutoDS, Doba, Faire) are **not repeated here**. They remain the fallback wherever a country has no good local option.

**WaggleKin products** (numbers used below): 1 calming donut bed · 2 self-cleaning slicker brush · 3 water fountain · 4 lick mat · 5 automatic feeder · 6 cat feather wand · 7 cooling gel mat · 8 2-in-1 travel bottle

**Bottom line:** in every market, **local pet-specialist dropshippers are rare** and most sell other people's brands (KONG, LickiMat, PetSafe, PawHut), not unbranded products you can sell as WaggleKin. The realistic setup is:
1. **CJdropshipping local warehouses** for the WaggleKin-branded basics (brush, lick mat, wand, bottle), as `suppliers.md` recommends.
2. **The local suppliers below** for bulky or electrical items (beds, fountains, feeders, cooling mats), where local stock and local plugs matter most.
3. Later, for best sellers, **your own stock in a local 3PL** (Southern Pet in Australia already offers this).

## 🇺🇸 United States

Context: duty-free de minimis ended on 29 Aug 2025, so cross-border parcels now pay duty plus brokerage at the door. **Use US-warehoused stock.** The genuine US pet distributors sell **name brands** at a set wholesale price, so your margin comes from your retail price, not from white-labelling.

- **Site:** [topdawg.com](https://www.topdawg.com/) · Fort Lauderdale, FL. A marketplace: products ship from many US supplier warehouses, so one cart can ship from several places.
- **Pet range:** 500k+ products overall with a dedicated pet department; versions of all 8 products are likely (not checked SKU by SKU).
- **Shopify:** ✅ [TopDawg Wholesale Dropshipping app](https://apps.shopify.com/topdawg) (5.0★, 21 reviews). API on Premier.
- **Cost (Nov 2026):**

  | Scale | $99.99 | $69.99/mo | 5,000 | 7% | $1.25 |
  | Premier | $199.99 | $139.99/mo | 25,000 | | $0.75 |

  Prices are per integration. Returns and restocking may fall on you.
- **Delivery:** tracking within 1 business day; transit depends on the supplier.
- **Blind / branded:** branded packing slips on **Scale and above**.
- **Apply:** sign up or install the app (7-day trial). Non-US eligibility isn't stated, so ask.

### Pet Wholesale USA (formerly Lee-Mar Pet)

- **Apply:** [online application](https://www.petwholesaleusa.com/pages/new-account-application.html). **Requires a verifiable US resale tax ID.** A Malta company would likely need a US state sales-tax permit first.

### Pet Stores USA (UTM Distributing)

- **Pet range:** 2,500+ name-brand items: beds (incl. cooling, bolster, "Calming" category), grooming, automatic feeding/watering, toys, travel. Likely covers **2, 5, 7, 8**, possibly 1 and 6. **No fountains or lick mats seen.**

- **Apply:** contact form, customerservice@petstoresusa.com, or 888-300-5139. Ask whether a Malta company is accepted.

### PetDropShipper
- **Site:** [petdropshipper.com](https://www.petdropshipper.com/) · Fairfield, New Jersey; one US warehouse.
- **Pet range:** 300+ brands (KONG, Drinkwell, Kaytee…). Likely covers **1, 2, 3 (Drinkwell fountains), 5, 6**, possibly 8; no lick mats or cooling mats seen.

- **Blind:** ✅ *(3rd-party, two sources)*.

## 🇨🇦 Canada

**The hard truth:** almost no Canadian pet distributor dropships for online stores. The researcher checked Pet Science, Can-Pet, Paw Pro Supply, Avapet, Olego, Maddie's, Anipet, Trident and CanDistribution. All are B2B wholesalers selling to physical shops. Pet Science explicitly excludes online-only stores, and Harbour Hound (the one pet specialist with a programme) is closed to new clients. Canadian brands in your range (Catit/Hagen fountains and feeders, Messy Mutts lick mats, Bowsers beds) sell **wholesale only**, mostly via Faire.

What *does* work from Canadian stock:

### Costway Canada
- **Site:** [costway.ca/points/dropship](https://www.costway.ca/points/dropship) · warehouses "east to west" across Canada.
- **Pet range:** mostly bulky items: calming couch bed (C$42.99), orthopedic beds, crates, cat trees. Covers **1 (partial)** only.
- **Shopify:** no app. Orders go in through a "Dropship Center" account; there is a daily stock/price data file.
- **Cost:** free. Discount off retail by last month's volume: **16%** (<20 orders), **18%** (20–39), **20%** (40+). MAP = Costway's own price, so margins are thin. No Amazon, Walmart, Wayfair, eBay, Home Depot or Lowe's.
- **Delivery:** ships next business day, 3–10 business days, UPS/FedEx/regional carriers; free standard shipping. No territories, PO boxes or many remote postcodes.
- **Blind:** ✅ "price tag and invoices will not be included".
- **Apply:** "Submit My Application", then email dropship.ca@costway.com for reseller e-certification.

### Aosom Canada (PawHut)
- **Site:** [aosom.ca/page/dropshipper](https://www.aosom.ca/page/dropshipper) · Markham, Ontario (office; warehouse locations unconfirmed).
- **Pet range:** PawHut automatic cat feeders (timer, Wi-Fi, camera) and cooling cots. Covers **5**, partly **7** and possibly **1**. Products are PawHut-branded.
- **Shopify:** no app; manual ordering (a data feed exists on the US programme).
- **Cost:** *(3rd-party)* no fee, no minimum. No Amazon/eBay/Wayfair resale.
- **Apply:** sign-up form, then approval. Ask whether a Malta company is accepted. Medium-low confidence until Aosom confirms its Canadian warehouses.

### DropCommerce (marketplace, Kelowna BC)
- **Site:** [dropcommerce.com](https://dropcommerce.com/) · 400+ suppliers, all US or Canada; pet is a category.
- **Shopify:** ✅ [DropCommerce app](https://apps.shopify.com/dropcommerce) (4.7★, ~130 reviews).
- **Cost:**

  | Plan | Price | Products | Notes |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Free | $0 | 25 | browse, samples |
  | Basic | $29/mo | 25 | |
  | Growth | $55/mo | 250 | branded invoicing |
  | Pro | $95/mo | unlimited | auto-fulfilment |

  No transaction fees; 14-day trial.
- **Delivery:** 2–7 days; set by each supplier.
- **Caveat:** no confirmed "ships from Canada" filter. Check each supplier's origin.

### AppScenic (marketplace with a Canadian pet supplier)
- **Site:** [appscenic.com/start-dropshipping-in-canada](https://appscenic.com/start-dropshipping-in-canada) · lists a Canadian **Pets** supplier (ID CA-2022-1562), 2–5 day delivery.
- **Shopify:** ✅ "DropShipping AI ‑ AppScenic" (3.9★, 48 reviews).
- **Cost:** you need at least **Standard $39/mo** to push products (Pro $79, Elite $99).
- **Caveat:** that supplier's catalogue and blind-shipping policy are unknown until you sign up.

**Dropped after checking (CA):** Harbour Hound Pet Distributing (closed to new clients; Canada-only proposals) · inQ Boutique (Doba listings ship from the **US** only) · "Leading High-Quality Pet Products" = Pet Science Ltd (excludes online-only stores) · CentralPet (US B2B portal, not Canadian).

**Canada gotchas:**
- **Tax:** goods sold from Canadian warehouses mean registering for normal GST/HST once sales pass C$30k. A Canadian supplier dropshipping for an unregistered non-resident must charge you GST/HST under the drop-shipment rules. Register early.
- **Language:** English + French labelling is required, and Quebec (Bill 96) also needs French product pages.
- **Electrical:** the fountain and feeder need CSA/cUL/cETL.
- **Shopify Collective doesn't work:** it requires the retailer and the supplier to be in the same country and currency.
- **Practical answer for products 2, 3, 4, 6, 8:** CJ's Canadian stock (reported in Vancouver/Toronto, 3–7 days via Canada Post), Syncee with "Pet Supplies + ships from Canada", or ship from US stock DDP (as `suppliers.md` suggests).

## 🇬🇧 United Kingdom

**Before anything else:** as a Malta company with UK stock, WaggleKin must **register for UK VAT before its first UK sale**. There is no threshold for non-established sellers. Some suppliers also ask for a UK VAT number and a GB EORI just to open an account.

- **Shopify:** ✅ [Avasam app](https://apps.shopify.com/avasam): automatic order routing and price sync.

- **Caveat:** the pricing page has stale "all suppliers are based in the USA" text. Ask which suppliers hold UK stock and whether a Malta company can join.

### MH Star UK (Aosom UK, PawHut brand): fountains and feeders
- **Site:** [mhstar.co.uk/pages/b2b-services](https://mhstar.co.uk/pages/b2b-services) · London HQ; fulfilment centres in Bury St Edmunds, Peterborough, Doncaster, Northampton and Bedford.

### Go Dropship

## 🇦🇺 Australia

**Registration is the main hurdle:** most Australian dropshippers want an **ABN**. Dropshipzone and dropXL are the easiest for a Malta company. Getting an ABN as a non-resident is possible once you're making sales connected with Australia; ask an accountant. Several suppliers quote prices **including GST**.

### Dropshipzone ⭐ biggest local platform
- **Site:** [dropshipzone.com.au](https://www.dropshipzone.com.au) · Hawthorn East, VIC. A marketplace: Australian suppliers must hold stock in Australia.
- **Pet range:** large pet category: calming beds (i.Pet, Fur King, YES4PETS, Pawfriends, Vaka) (**1**), automatic fountains (**3**), automatic feeders (**5**). Others are likely given the catalogue size.
- **Shopify:** ✅ [Dropshipzone app](https://apps.shopify.com/newaim_app) (4.3★), plus API and CSV.
- **Cost:** **free**, no subscription. Pay per order (card, PayPal, credit limit); prices include GST; no MOQ; bulk discounts at 10+ units.
- **Delivery:** dispatched in 1–2 business days; Australia only.
- **Blind:** partial. No contact with your customer, but the **label shows the supplier as sender**. No custom packaging.
- **Caveats:**
  - Some sellers are **overseas suppliers**, so filter for Australian stock on each SKU.
  - Sign-up mentions a "valid local business number for international partners". Confirm that a Malta registration or VAT number is enough.

### Southern Pet Supplies (formerly Agline) ⭐ best pet specialist
- **Site:** [southernpetsupplies.com.au/dropship](https://www.southernpetsupplies.com.au/dropship/) · Adelaide, SA; own warehouse.
- **Pet range:** thousands of pet SKUs, all third-party brands. Covers:
  - **4** (LickiMat)

  - Slow/enrichment feeders (Outward Hound, Nina Ottosson, PetSafe)
  - Beds (**1**, calming not confirmed), brushes (**2**), travel (**8**), cat toys (**6**)
- **Shopify:** no app or feed; order through the trade portal.
- **Cost:** **no signup, account or transaction fees, no minimum.** Freight by published table (ex GST):

  | VIC/NSW capitals | $7.60 |

  It also runs a **3PL for your own stock**: inbound $10/carton, storage $12.50/pallet/month, pick and pack from $1.79/item.
- **Apply:** [application](https://www.southernpetsupplies.com.au/applications/) → "eCommerce store". **Requires:**
  - an ABN, plus a registered business name matching your trading name
