# WaggleKin: Local Dropshippers by Country

**Prepared:** 11 November 2026 · **Markets:** US, Canada, UK, Australia, EU/Malta
**Companion to:** [`suppliers.md`](suppliers.md) (global platforms, product costs, compliance and tax)
**Scope:** Research only. Nothing was signed up for, contacted or bought.

> **How this list was built.** One researcher per market searched directories, Shopify App Store listings, local-language queries and supplier websites. A separate fact-checker then tried to *disprove* every candidate: wholesale-only, no stock in the country, no real pet range, dead site, or wrong facts. Only the suppliers that held up are listed below. The ones dropped, and why, are at the end of each section so you don't waste time on them. Most terms come from the suppliers' own pages. Where a fact comes only from a third-party roundup it is marked *(3rd-party)*. **Confirm fees, stock and eligibility for a Malta company with each supplier before you list products.**
>
> The global platforms in `suppliers.md` (CJdropshipping, Syncee, Spocket, Zendrop, DSers, BigBuy, AutoDS, Doba, Faire) are **not repeated here**. They remain the fallback wherever a country has no good local option.

**Bottom line:** in every market, **local pet-specialist dropshippers are rare** and most sell other people's brands (KONG, LickiMat, PetSafe, PawHut), not unbranded products you can sell as WaggleKin. The realistic setup is:
1. **CJdropshipping local warehouses** for the WaggleKin-branded basics (brush, lick mat, wand, bottle), as `suppliers.md` recommends.

## 🇨🇦 Canada

**The hard truth:** almost no Canadian pet distributor dropships for online stores. The researcher checked Pet Science, Can-Pet, Paw Pro Supply, Avapet, Olego, Maddie's, Anipet, Trident and CanDistribution. All are B2B wholesalers selling to physical shops. Pet Science explicitly excludes online-only stores, and Harbour Hound (the one pet specialist with a programme) is closed to new clients. Canadian brands in your range (Catit/Hagen fountains and feeders, Messy Mutts lick mats, Bowsers beds) sell **wholesale only**, mostly via Faire.

What *does* work from Canadian stock:

**Canada gotchas:**
- **Tax:** goods sold from Canadian warehouses mean registering for normal GST/HST once sales pass C$30k. A Canadian supplier dropshipping for an unregistered non-resident must charge you GST/HST under the drop-shipment rules. Register early.
- **Language:** English + French labelling is required, and Quebec (Bill 96) also needs French product pages.
- **Electrical:** the fountain and feeder need CSA/cUL/cETL.
- **Shopify Collective doesn't work:** it requires the retailer and the supplier to be in the same country and currency.
- **Practical answer for products 2, 3, 4, 6, 8:** CJ's Canadian stock (reported in Vancouver/Toronto, 3–7 days via Canada Post), Syncee with "Pet Supplies + ships from Canada", or ship from US stock DDP (as `suppliers.md` suggests).

## 🇪🇺 European Union & Malta

**Two things to know first:**
- **Malta is the weak point.** No Maltese pet business dropships; they all supply local shops only. Most EU dropshippers either don't list Malta (Petdiscount, vidaXL, Hertwill) or haven't confirmed it. **Get a written Malta shipping quote before you switch the Malta market on.**
- **You need a VIES-valid Maltese VAT number** (registered under **Article 10**, not the Article 11 small-business scheme). Almost every EU supplier checks it. Selling from NL, DE or PL stock to consumers in other EU countries brings **OSS** reporting; see `suppliers.md` §4.2.

Unlike the US/UK/AU, the **Netherlands and Poland have real pet-specialist wholesalers that dropship**.
